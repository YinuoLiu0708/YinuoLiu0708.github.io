**Book Chapter** in *Handbook for Behavioral Parent Training*. Washington, DC: American Psychological Association.

**Abstract**

Understanding the impact of behavior disorders on underresourced families through both theoretical frameworks and empirical findings highlights the need for comprehensive intervention strategies that address not only individual behaviors but also the broader environmental contexts influencing these families. Engaging underresourced families in evidence-based interventions is critical, and behavioral parent training (BPT) has a more robust evidence base than any other intervention to date for early-onset behavior disorders. Building on the literature demonstrating an increased vulnerability for behavior disorders in underresourced families, this chapter navigates the intricacies of facilitating the delivery of BPT techniques to meet the unique needs of underresourced families, offering insights into fostering engagement and achieving meaningful outcomes. It choses to use the term underresourced families to summarize themes across this literature. The chapter introduces one example of a technology-enhanced BPT program involving digital tools: technology-enhanced helping the noncompliant child.
